The model, textures, and images are dedicated to the public domain to the
extent possible under law. Origin disclosure from the creator: AI-assisted;
the base mesh and textures were generated with an AI image-to-3D tool, then
cleaned, scaled, textured, and checked in Blender.

The source GLB has 751,096 triangles and includes walk/run animation clips.
This project uses a distance-based lower-detail mesh for the character.
