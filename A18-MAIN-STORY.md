# 青埔的未寄之信 — continuous local main story

An original, warm neighbourhood fiction, with seven ordered chapters. NPCs and the letter are fictional; temple facts and landmark positions remain grounded in the district references.

1. The community secretary entrusts an unfinished letter to the player.
2. The station service partner recognises the first clue.
3. A photograph in the civic-centre exhibition points to Gongqi Park.
4. A page at the Qingsheng cultural exhibit gives the next part of the letter.
5. The park neighbour asks the player to choose what makes a place feel like home; that choice changes the final letter.
6. At the Dayuan/Ksitigarbha observation point the player writes a wish for the future.
7. Return the completed letter to the secretary; a framed letter appears in the living-room souvenir niche.

Each chapter requires the actual nearby NPC/object and the previous chapter.
