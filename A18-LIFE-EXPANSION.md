# A18 lived-in district and enterable venues

Outcome: make the existing A18 district recognizable at street level and give it destinations with real gameplay inside. Preserve home/community continuity, 100 km/h Model Y and saved rewards. Continue to use OSM positions and footprints instead of adding arbitrary building masses.

Exterior scope: differentiate the mapped COZZI Blu/Xpark/Shin Kong complex on 春德路, open-sided station canopies (the previous roof-as-six-storey-building interpretation is incorrect), P3 parking facade, mapped crossing/bus-stop/bicycle-rental context, outlet storefront signage and pedestrian landscaping. Correct canopies in both rendering and ground collision. Retain supplied floor/height data; facade interpretation is explicitly approximate. Do not present proposed/unverified construction as an opened venue.

Eight enterable spaces: THSR concourse, IKEA sample showroom, mapped Gloria COACH retail space, mapped Gloria 春水堂 tea space, water-centre learning pavilion, COZZI lobby, Xpark-inspired marine gallery, Shin Kong cinema foyer/small screening space. These are original game interiors, not surveyed replicas, actual venue services, real ticketing or hotel booking. Entrances correspond to their mapped buildings; water-centre learning pavilion is an interpretive game space. Only the visited room is built and rendered. Entry requires leaving the vehicle; opening/closing doors, a short transition and a consistent outdoor return pose prevent teleporting or trapping the player in the building footprint.

Characters: venue-specific staff and visitors (at least ten across the eight spaces), dialogue, wayfinding and six quests. Activities include finding/returning a traveller's ticket, preparing a specified tea order, adjusting a display lamp/chair, marine recycling sorting, setting up a projector and delivering a cafe postcard to the concierge. Correct actions change physical objects and persistent inventory/progress. Quest rewards use the existing home memory wall. Prior claimed rewards and Sweet Home figure remain unlocked.

Contracts: B1_A18_LIFE exposes synchronous quest/action metadata and lazy zone definitions before B1_COMMUNITY initializes. It resolves real exterior entries after B1_CITY.ready, attaches game recording/save hooks, and stores its state in the existing city save object. B1_COMMUNITY delegates venue targets/dialogue and ensures lazy scenes before travel. City map includes enterable venues and navigation while retaining the five main destinations. Existing public city methods remain compatible.

Acceptance: every venue has a legal exterior entry, legal indoor spawn, physical exit, return pose outside obstacles and parked cars, NPC and meaningful activity; cars cannot enter or carry speed inside. Partial activity inventory survives serialization, correct completions are idempotent and existing rewards persist. Map navigation reaches each entrance. Static scene pieces are batched; inactive characters/rooms do not tick. Normal low-quality outdoor views remain below 300 draw calls/500k triangles, indoor views below 180 calls, on the test device; no universal FPS guarantee.

Validation: Node polygon/road audit including canopy change; browser tests for real E interaction, eight door/return paths, gameplay/quests/save round-trip, vehicle preservation, low-quality budgets, screenshots and native WebGPU/WebGL. Repeat focused published-build verification after commit/deploy.

## Geographic orientation correction

The original city conversion sent south-positive OSM Y directly into the north-positive indoor CAD Y, reflecting the district. `studio-city-geo.js` now owns conversion: map `[east,south]` metres → CAD `[2400+100*east,800-100*south]` centimetres. The existing indoor `W` conversion is preserved. Road angles, facades, roof vertices, collision bounds, pedestrians, entrances, headings and map arrows follow this convention. Geometry is rebuilt rather than reflected with a negative mesh scale; readable sign faces are preserved. North-facing cameras place IKEA east/right of the HSR station and Gloria to its west/left.

City saves carry `geoVersion:2`. Older parked vehicle positions reflect around CAD Y=800 and their headings are converted once; valid migrated positions apply whether data finished loading before or after save restoration. Existing claimed rewards and home figure remain sticky. Map UI displays north/east markers.

Performance refinement: the community exterior's repeated railings/fins are instanced by material, and distant parked vehicles are hidden by quality-dependent distance. Venue scenes are built lazily, static furniture is material-batched, and only the active venue animates fish, water and projection.
