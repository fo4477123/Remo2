Bria and Cavira now use separate body cross sections and front/rear depth profiles. These are controls traced by observation from photographs, not factory CAD or surveyed measurements.

Changes:
- Removed lamp-driven automatic raising of front/rear panels on these two cars.
- Independent lower sill, door hollow, shoulder and hood crown sections.
- Curved S-Duct opening with separate dimensions for each car, shared panel boundaries, painted inner walls and a recessed throat.
- Hood edges meet the fender sections. Removed obsolete interior tubes exposed by the new door hollow.
- Cavira rear optical housing, thin upper guide, lower return and individual reflector elements replace the solid red panel. Rear lettering fitted on both cars.
- Added a hood / S-Duct inspection camera to the gallery.

Visual fidelity still requires refinement: the models remain photo-derived procedural reconstructions. Automated geometry and interaction checks cannot certify an exact factory-body match.

## Panel follow-up

Independent longitudinal bonnet and canopy stations now control Bria and Cavira. The lower front intake is triangulated as a hole in the painted shell, with inner walls and recessed grille components. Spoilers have curved upper and lower surfaces, closed end caps and rear aero blades. Door hollow and shoulder positions vary along the body length.

Cavira rear lower valance, silver lower edge and plate height were additionally checked against a full rear view from the launch event: https://www.mobile01.com/topicdetail.php?f=1562&t=7263119 . This supplements the cropped factory rear photograph; it is not a factory CAD source.

The gallery provides front, side and rear orthographic views and a panel visibility control for inspecting the complete silhouette. These views make remaining mismatches visible and do not constitute proof of exact reproduction.

## Forward S-Duct inlet repair

The previous bonnet outlet was a pocket behind an intact front fascia. The upper forward mouth is now a second hole in the triangulated bumper shell, connected to the bonnet outlet by upper, lower and side tunnel surfaces. The old pocket floor is removed. A frontal ray at the inlet midpoint must travel beyond the fascia plane before hitting the inner channel.

The two outboard headlamp housings no longer span the center mouth. Bria uses vertically stacked reflector/lens units; Cavira uses horizontal pairs. Running-light ribbons remain separate from the housings. The projector meshes use the existing headlight batching/clone path.
