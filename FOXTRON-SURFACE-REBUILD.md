# Foxtron surface correction, 2026-11-06

Bria and Cavira now use separate body cross sections and front/rear depth profiles. These are controls traced by observation from photographs, not factory CAD or surveyed measurements.

References inspected:
- Bria official 2026 brochure, pages 2–5: https://www-assets.cloud.foxtronev.com/files/specifications/bria/en/FOXTRON-BRIA-Brochure-EN-2026-v2.pdf
- Cavira official side: https://www-assets.cloud.foxtronev.com/images/models/explore/cavira/innerAndOuter/outer.webp
- Cavira official front: https://www-assets.cloud.foxtronev.com/images/models/explore/cavira/innerAndOuter/OuterDetail4.webp
- Cavira official rear: https://www-assets.cloud.foxtronev.com/images/models/explore/cavira/detailInside/main.webp

Changes:
- Removed lamp-driven automatic raising of front/rear panels on these two cars.
- Independent lower sill, door hollow, shoulder and hood crown sections.
- Curved S-Duct opening with separate dimensions for each car, shared panel boundaries, painted inner walls and a recessed throat.
- Hood edges meet the fender sections. Removed obsolete interior tubes exposed by the new door hollow.
- Cavira rear optical housing, thin upper guide, lower return and individual reflector elements replace the solid red panel. Rear lettering fitted on both cars.
- Added a hood / S-Duct inspection camera to the gallery.

Visual fidelity still requires refinement: the models remain photo-derived procedural reconstructions. Automated geometry and interaction checks cannot certify an exact factory-body match.

## Panel follow-up

Independent longitudinal bonnet and canopy stations now control Bria and Cavira. The lower front intake is triangulated as a hole in the painted shell, with inner walls and recessed grille components. Spoilers have curved upper and lower surfaces, closed end caps and rear aero blades. Door hollow and shoulder positions vary along the body length.

Cavira rear lower valance, silver lower edge and plate height were additionally checked against a full rear view from the launch event: https://www.mobile01.com/topicdetail.php?f=1562&t=7263119 . This supplements the cropped factory rear photograph; it is not a factory CAD source.

The gallery provides front, side and rear orthographic views and a panel visibility control for inspecting the complete silhouette. These views make remaining mismatches visible and do not constitute proof of exact reproduction.

## Forward S-Duct inlet repair

The previous bonnet outlet was a pocket behind an intact front fascia. The upper forward mouth is now a second hole in the triangulated bumper shell, connected to the bonnet outlet by upper, lower and side tunnel surfaces. The old pocket floor is removed. A frontal ray at the inlet midpoint must travel beyond the fascia plane before hitting the inner channel.

The two outboard headlamp housings no longer span the center mouth. Bria uses vertically stacked reflector/lens units; Cavira uses horizontal pairs. Running-light ribbons remain separate from the housings. The projector meshes use the existing headlight batching/clone path.

## Ramp and rear sail correction

Rechecked the Bria official brochure pages 2-4 and Cavira official side/front photographs. The forward channel now reaches the bonnet outlet directly, without the vertical pocket walls. Its curved painted floor uses cavity shading. The badge-bearing bumper ramp has a recessed upper section; the FOXTRON decal geometry conforms to that surface instead of occupying a vertical plane. Projectors use separate rounded rectangular optical elements.

Rear sail panels now have independent slanted boundaries in the curved side glazing coordinates. Bria has its opaque patterned panel; Cavira has a triangulated quarter-light aperture. Removed overlapping generic rear pillar tubes and relocated the Bria graphic onto its new panel. Analytic bumper normals remove triangulation fan highlights around the intake.

These are still photo-derived approximations; body silhouette, optics and material fidelity are not equivalent to manufacturer CAD or the imported Model 3. Gallery front/profile/hood views expose the current result for comparison.

## Surface and optical fit follow-up

The central inlet recess now blends out before the outboard lamp pockets instead of distorting the entire nose. Front tessellation bounds edge lengths in centimetres to improve narrow-triangle curvature. Rounded projector faces and their reflector borders follow the front surface. The rear spoiler and brake strip now share the revised downward trailing profile.

Rear side and hatch glazing have separate privacy tint. The black canopy has a clear-coated finish; body paint uses a less metallic base. Machined wheel faces no longer overlap the black casting, and radial depth gives the spokes a dished section. Added nose and C-pillar gallery close-ups and a focused --foxtron QA mode.

The remaining broad body silhouette and small trim proportions are still approximate; this update does not establish a factory-accurate reconstruction.

## Contour and shell closure follow-up

Rechecked the official Cavira side/front and Bria brochure views against gallery renders. Lowered the outboard DRL to follow the bonnet leading edge and added its smoked bridge. Bria now includes the outboard black air-curtain regions. Replaced the cylindrical rocker and arch trim with formed surface strips.

The flatter arch trim exposed a pre-existing open wheel house; added interior backing behind all four articulated wheels. Regression rays now check that the sky cannot be seen through the gap above each tyre. Removed the bonnet fender-crown function from the rear deck and made the glazing crown taper at both ends, eliminating painted lobes intersecting the rear windscreen.

The models remain procedural approximations. In particular, the exact bumper curvature, door sculpting and rear optical housings still need more faithful reconstruction. Geometry validation does not establish visual parity with the real vehicles.

## Shared rear-glazing boundary

Follow-up to the reported body-line and rear-glass mismatch: the shell used longitudinal end contraction while the glazing remained in uncontracted coordinates. Rear glazing and sail-panel coordinates now use the same end contraction, blended out towards the roof. Their lower edge follows the deck height with a small seal offset. The window sill interpolates full 3D edge coordinates instead of leaving its longitudinal component fixed.

Sectioned side panels now use one moderate door hollow and wheel flare, rather than compounding generic sine displacement, deep panel offsets and shoulder ridges. Rounded upper section stations replace the flat ledge, and the side panels use their own geometric normals. Focused geometry, wheel-house, duct, lettering, LOD and wheel animation checks passed; rear/side screenshots inspected. This repairs the structural mismatch but is not a claim of exact OEM body fidelity.

## Rear optics, glazing detail, fender openings and bonnet sections

Added surface-aligned rear ceramic borders, defroster wires, rubber lower seal and wiper. Rear FOXTRON lettering now conforms to the tailgate. Bria matrix elements have rounded corners; Cavira includes separate oval reflector elements within an extended optical housing. Small elements use direct triangulation instead of full bumper subdivision.

Each side shell now has exact grid boundaries around two fender slots, with return walls and a recessed backing. Slot positions and dimensions are provisional photo-derived controls, not confirmed factory measurements. They need further source comparison for exact fidelity. Independent transverse bonnet sections form a broad crown and shallow side valleys, flattening at the leading bridge; these replace the common Gaussian fender hump. The existing through S-duct is retained.

Focused gallery QA passed for finite geometry, four wheels, duct clearance, lettering, wheel-house closure, LOD and rolling. Side, hood and rear renders inspected. Shape similarity remains incomplete.

## Bonnet seams and front identity insert

Rechecked Bria brochure page 5 close-up. Removed the generic raised painted hood tubes and used fine panel shut-lines outside the S-duct outlet. Added the bonnet leading shut-line and a small dark central identity insert with metallic graphic strokes; the DRL halves stop at the insert. Focused gallery checks passed and the front close-up was inspected. These are photo-derived interpretations and remain visually short of the factory vehicle.

## FOXTRON letter spacing

Replaced font-dependent space characters with seven independently positioned glyphs on the existing wordmark texture. Letter centres are evenly spaced at 58 pixels; the front and rear surface-fitted panels therefore display a wider wordmark without changing the glyph height. Focused gallery checks passed and nose preview inspected.

## Charge flap, rear door, wheel and A-pillar correction

Charge-flap geometry now uses a 16 by 14 cm rounded rectangle in physical side coordinates before fitting to the body. This prevents changing panel height from stretching its outline. Rear door trailing edges now rise around the rear arch using independent stations; the centre seam and lower door seam meet the sill.

Cavira wheel casting now has ten single machined faces instead of twenty apparent branches. Reduced hub-cap/rim-lip proportions, reduced brake-disc radius and expanded hidden wheel-house backing. Added broad painted A-pillar surfaces on the windscreen and side glazing, replacing the isolated thin front tube.

Both front and rear fender slots were widened following the user correction and recessed to 7 cm. Their dimensions remain provisional pending exact reference matching. Added a charge-flap gallery camera. Focused geometry, duct, wheel-house, wheel animation and LOD checks passed; charge/wheel/side/front images inspected. This update does not claim factory CAD accuracy.

## Wheel hardware and flush handles

Wheel castings and machined faces now use closed bevelled extrusions with separate depths and a radial dish. Added centre-cap lettering and tyre valve stems. The brake calipers are attached to the stationary body group rather than the rolling wheel group. Bria flush handles use fine rounded panel outlines and surface-fitted faces instead of thick protruding boxes.

Focused Bria/Cavira checks passed for finite geometry, duct clearance, wheel-house closure, LOD and wheel animation. Wheel and charge-side previews inspected. Shape fidelity is still incomplete and no factory CAD equivalence is claimed.

## Optical covers and wheel finishes

Added formed transparent headlamp and tail-lamp covers with narrow return walls. The covers follow the existing optical outlines and remain distinct from the opaque housings and emissive internals. Wheel machined faces and castings now have separate clear-coated metal and dark paint finishes. Focused Bria/Cavira geometry, duct, wheel-house, LOD and rolling checks passed; nose, rear and wheel previews inspected. This is a material/detail improvement rather than proof of accurate overall vehicle reproduction.
